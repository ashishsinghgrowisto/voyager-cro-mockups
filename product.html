Description ▾
The Step-In Plush is a fuss-free vest-style harness — just step in, clip and go. Made from stylish suede lined with soft plush, it keeps your pet warm and snug on brisk walks. Three security features and two reflective bands work together to protect your pet on every adventure.
Specifications ▾
  • Material: Suede outer, plush lining
  • Closure: Step-in, dual buckle lock
  • Sizes: XS (13–14.5") to XL (20.5–23")
  • Reflective: 2 side safety bands
  • Care: Hand wash, air dry
Features & Safety ▾
  • Triple-lock security system
  • Padded chest plate for comfort
  • D-ring for leash attachment
Sizing & Care ▾
Measure your pet's chest girth before ordering. Runs true to size. Hand wash cold, air dry.

Customer FAQs

How do I choose the right size?▾
Measure your pet's chest girth and match it to our size chart. If between sizes, size up. Use the “Find my size” tool above.
Is the suede material machine washable?▾
We recommend hand washing in cold water and air drying to preserve the suede and plush lining.
How long does shipping take?▾
Orders ship within 24 hours. Standard delivery is 3–5 business days; free over $30.
What is your return policy?▾
