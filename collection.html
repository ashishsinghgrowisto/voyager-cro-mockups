Explore Our Step-In Harnesses

Picture this: a dog harness or cat harness that you can simply clip to your furry friend, without the usual fuss. That's what we offer here at Voyager — premium step-in harnesses that guarantee your animals stay snug and secure, no matter where they go or how much they wriggle around.

A World Of Comfort And Safety

The comfort of your pet is priority number one, and that's where our harnesses shine. Crafted from soft, breathable material, a step-in harness is like a gentle hug around your pet's body, keeping them cool and comfortable all day long. The safety component is also a massive benefit. If you ever take your pet out for an evening stroll, the reflective bands catch the light, so they're visible to cars and pedestrians at all times. With the added bonus of a hook and loop fastener, our harnesses mean you never have to worry about your pet escaping from your side.

Vet-Recommended For Health And Comfort

Vets often recommend this type of harness, especially for smaller pets. The step-in design is a blessing for those with sensitive backs or necks. It evenly distributes pressure, making it a safer option compared to traditional collars or harnesses. It's particularly helpful for pets who tend to pull a lot or have respiratory issues since it doesn't compromise their ability to move or breathe freely.

Best-Selling Favorites

The popularity of the Voyager step in air harness isn't just about how it looks. It's about how it elevates walks into a relaxed, enjoyable experience — every single time. Unlike other options on the market, our harness stands out for its user-friendly design, proving that something this well-made can make a big difference. Simply grab the harness, step in, and go.

Starting Young With Harness Training

Responsibly using a harness with your pet at a young age — as early as 8 to 10 weeks — sets the stage for a lifetime of safe and pleasant walks. The step-in harnesses at Voyager are ideal for this, offering a gentle introduction to leash training. It's less intimidating for a young pet and helps build their confidence, making those early walks a fun and positive experience.

Easy Care For Lasting Comfort

Caring for your harness is as easy as a stroll through the park. A simple hand wash with mild soap and water, followed by air drying, keeps it in top condition. It's designed to endure the rigors of daily use while still staying fresh. Our harnesses are made to be a long-lasting companion for you and your pet — no matter where life takes you.

FAQs

What Materials Are Your Harnesses Made Out Of?

Our lightweight harnesses are made from a soft and sturdy air mesh that's both flexible and breathable.

Are There Any Security Features?

The hook and loop fastener, buckle, and double D-Rings work together to protect your pet and keep them securely by your side.

Can I Use A Step-In Harness For Daily Walks?

Absolutely! Our step-in harnesses are designed for everyday use, making walks with your pet stress-free and effortless.
